⚡ Power Systems

Three Phase Power

Power Factor

Power Factor = Real Power / Apparent Power

HVDC

High Voltage Direct Current transmission is used for efficient long-distance power transmission.

Load Flow

Load-flow analysis determines bus voltage, power flow and system losses.

Fault Analysis

Common faults include three-phase, line-to-ground, line-to-line and double-line-to-ground faults.

🔧 Electrical Machines

Transformer

A transformer transfers electrical energy between circuits through electromagnetic induction.

Induction Motor

An induction motor operates using electromagnetic induction and is widely used in industry.

Synchronous Machine

A synchronous machine operates at synchronous speed and can work as a generator or motor.

DC Machine

DC machines can operate as motors or generators.

🔌 Power Electronics

Rectifier

Converts AC power into DC power.

Inverter

Converts DC power into AC power.

Chopper

Converts fixed DC voltage into a controlled DC voltage.

IGBT

An insulated-gate bipolar transistor used for high-power switching applications.

SCR

A thyristor device commonly used for controlled rectification and power control.

☀️ Renewable Energy

Solar PV

Solar photovoltaic systems convert sunlight directly into electrical energy.

Wind Energy

Wind turbines convert the kinetic energy of wind into electrical power.

Battery Energy Storage

BESS stores electrical energy and supplies it when required.

Microgrid

A microgrid combines generation, loads, storage and control systems.

🛡️ Protection & Safety

MCB

Protects circuits against overload and short circuit.

MCCB

A circuit breaker used for higher current protection and industrial applications.

RCCB

Detects residual current and provides earth-leakage protection.

Earthing

Provides a low-impedance path for fault current to improve electrical safety.

📡 Smart Grid & IoT

Smart Meter

Measures electrical energy and can communicate measurement information.

IoT Monitoring

Sensors can monitor voltage, current, temperature and equipment condition.

Predictive Maintenance

Equipment measurements can be analyzed to identify potential maintenance requirements.
